# Goethe word list check

Wortwerk's 1,194 words compared with the official Goethe-Institut word lists:
- **A1:** *Start Deutsch 1 Wortliste* (VS_02, about 650 entries).
- **A2:** *Goethe-Zertifikat A2 Wortliste* (A2_Wortliste_03_200616, about 1,300 entries).

Words were matched on the bare word, ignoring the article, *sich* and plural markers. Checked 2026-10-28.

## Changes made after this check

- **Relabelled the 30 "We say A2, Goethe A1" words to A1.** IDs are unchanged. *der Kühlschrank* (69) keeps its slot in the first article round, so saved rounds resume where they left off.
- **Added 228 missing Goethe nouns (113 A1, 115 A2)** as IDs 1194–1421, in existing topics. Each new word has an English meaning that no other word shares.
  - Skipped: nouns with two genders, plural-only words, phrases (*Wiedersehen*), a country, an obsolete word (*Fax*), parsing noise (*Bogen*), duplicates of words we already have (*Pass* / *Reisepass*, *Disko*, *Stockwerk*), and feminine forms whose masculine form is already in the list.
- **Moved 134 of the 142 "We say A1, Goethe A2 only" words to A2.** Kept at A1:
  - *gern*, which is a Goethe A1 headword written *gern(e)*;
  - prepositions, pronouns and question words (*hinter*, *neben*, *außer*, *etwas*, *jemand*, *niemand*, *welcher*). Goethe lists these in its A1 grammar section rather than the word list.

  The article drill's first round is now pinned to fixed IDs, so this relabel doesn't change it. Every grammar topic's first round was compared with the last commit and is unchanged.
- **Added 161 missing verbs, adjectives, adverbs and function words (44 A1, 117 A2)** as IDs 1422–1582, for example *duschen*, *träumen*, *verschieben*, *sich kümmern*, *dringend*, *vorsichtig*, *montags* to *sonntags*, *wegen*.
  - Inflected forms in the list (*bewirbt*, *vergisst*) were mapped to their infinitives; most of those we already had.
  - Skipped grammar words the drills already cover (*mein*, *dich*, *kein*), interjections (*ja*, *hallo*, *tschüss*), comparatives (*besser*) and synonyms we already have (*nirgends* / *nirgendwo*).
- **Raised the progress save limit from 64 KB to 128 KB.** With 1,583 words, a fully learned account needs about 77 KB.
- The vocabulary now has 1,583 words: 731 A1 and 852 A2.
- The figures below are from before these changes.

## What it means

- **Genders are clean.** Every noun that has an article in the Goethe lists matches ours.
- **30 words are filed too high.** Words like *fahren*, *fliegen*, *die E-Mail*, *das Internet* and *die Polizei* are core A1 in Goethe's list. Moving them to A1 is safe: the level is only a label, and IDs stay the same.
- **142 "A1" words are A2 by Goethe's standard.** Examples: *der Stuhl*, *das Fenster*, *die Tür*, *warm*, *kalt*, clothes and many food words. Goethe's A1 list is deliberately small, so these aren't wrong for beginners. Relabelling them changes the "% of A1" figure and the A1-first order of new words in guided practice. That's a product choice.
- **364 words are in neither list.** Most are useful topic words (food, household, body parts: *Käse*, *Zwiebel*, *Handtuch*, *Knie*), which is fine for practice. Some "A2" words read as B1: *die Erfahrung*, *der Lebenslauf*, *zuverlässig*, *obwohl*, *nachdem*, *trotzdem*. Consider marking those B1 or keeping them as extras.
- **The bigger gap is high-frequency words we lack.** About 121 A1 nouns are missing, including *das Essen*, *die Zeit*, *der Mensch*, *das Problem*, *die Farbe*, *der Hunger*, *der Durst*, *die Hilfe*, *das Geschenk*, *die Freizeit*, *das Hobby*, *der Vorname*, *die Nummer*, *die Kleidung*, *der Eingang* and *die Speisekarte*. A similar set is missing at A2: *die Idee*, *die Geschichte*, *das Messer*, *die Gabel*, *der Löffel*, *die Gesundheit*, *der Keller*. Adding them means appending new IDs from 1194 with English meanings, topics and parts of speech.

## Limits

- The text was extracted from two-column PDFs. Headwords were pulled by pattern, so a few may be missed or picked up from example sentences.
- Gaps were counted for **nouns only**, because only an article reliably marks a headword. Missing verbs, adjectives and function words weren't counted.
- The Goethe lists cover exam vocabulary, not all useful words, so "not listed" doesn't mean wrong.
- Numbers, ordinals, pronouns (*du*, *ihr*, *wen*, *wem*) and *der Samstag* (written *Samstag/Sonnabend*) sit in Goethe's number and grammar sections, which weren't parsed. They show up as "in neither list" but are A1. That's about 50 of the 156 A1 words in that group.
- The full lists of missing Goethe words aren't reproduced here because the lists are the Goethe-Institut's publication. Rerun the comparison to get them while adding words.
